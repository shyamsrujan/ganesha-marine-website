The Autonagar workshop

02 / Workshop

Foundation structures fabricated for L&T Rambilli, and general machinery at the Autonagar yard.

Our tied-up workshop at B Block, Autonagar, Visakhapatnam has open and covered fabrication space, equipped for hull and structural steel work up to 20mm plate thickness.

  • Hydraulic press 100 tons
  • Plate rolling machine up to 20 mm
  • Hydra crane 12 tons
  • Welding SMAW / MIG
  • Radial drilling machine  
  • Lathe  
  • Gas cutting & grinding standard fabrication set

Ship design and stability calculations are handled through our design tie-up with Vizag Marine Consultants, Visakhapatnam.
